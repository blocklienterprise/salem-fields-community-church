Privacy Policy

Salem Fields Community Church – Internal Accounting Application

Last Updated: 01/28/2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Salem Fields Community Church (“Church,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, and protects information accessed through our internal accounting application (“Application”).

1. Scope

This Application is for internal church use only and is not a public consumer application.

2. Information We Access

When connected to Intuit QuickBooks, the Application may access:
	•	Financial transactions
	•	Sales receipts
	•	Invoices and payments
	•	Customer and vendor records
	•	Account balances and reports

This data is accessed only as necessary to support internal accounting, reporting, and financial administration.

3. How Information Is Used

Information accessed through the Application is used exclusively for:
	•	Church accounting and bookkeeping
	•	Financial reporting and reconciliation
	•	Internal audits and administrative oversight
	•	Compliance with financial and legal obligations

4. Data Sharing

The Church does not sell, rent, or share QuickBooks data with third parties. Data is accessed and used solely by authorized church personnel.

5. Data Storage and Security

Reasonable administrative and technical safeguards are in place to protect financial data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse. Access to the Application is restricted to authorized individuals only.

6. Data Retention

Data is retained only for as long as necessary to fulfill internal accounting and administrative purposes or as required by law.

7. Revoking Access

QuickBooks access may be revoked at any time through Intuit QuickBooks account settings. Once access is revoked, the Application will no longer retrieve data.

8. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Any data processed relates solely to church financial operations.

9. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated periodically to reflect operational or regulatory changes. Updates will be posted to this page.
